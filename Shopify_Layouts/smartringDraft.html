Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before purchasing your HealthSync SmartRing.

We provide a printable sizing guide and step-by-step instructions to help you measure your finger accurately. If you're between sizes, we recommend sizing up for comfort. You can also visit our sizing guide page for a virtual sizing assistant.

Our warranty covers manufacturing defects and normal wear and tear for two years from purchase. This includes battery performance degradation below 70% capacity, sensor malfunctions, and material defects. The warranty does not cover accidental damage or loss.

You can return your HealthSync SmartRing within 30 days of receiving it for a full refund, no questions asked. The ring must be in its original condition with all accessories included. We provide a prepaid return shipping label for your convenience.

The HealthSync app works with iOS 14+ (iPhone 8 and newer) and Android 10+ devices with Bluetooth 5.0 or higher. The app is not currently available for Windows phones or tablets without Bluetooth capability.

With normal use (continuous heart rate, sleep, and activity tracking), the HealthSync SmartRing typically lasts 7 days on a single charge. Charging takes about 90 minutes from 0-100% using the included wireless charging dock.
